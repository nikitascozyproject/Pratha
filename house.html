THE HOUSE

A tradition, carried forward.

Pratha means a tradition, a practice passed down and carried forward. Not nostalgia. Something alive.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Indian craft, without the museum glass.

PRATHA works with textiles, materials and craft traditions for their meaning, history and ability to live naturally in the present.

We are interested in the things that acquire character with use: cloth that softens, objects that gather stories, patterns that remain recognisably Indian without becoming a costume.

A SLOWER WAY OF LIVING

The beauty is in the quieter details.

Old havelis. Worn stone. Tulsi in a courtyard. A house working late into the afternoon. These are part of PRATHA's visual language because they are part of the life around us.
