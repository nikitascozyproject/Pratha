Objects that stay.

Why we choose fewer things, made to last, over trend-led interiors.

Some things become more beautiful because they remain. A worn edge, a softened textile, a surface changed by years of touch — these are signs of life, not flaws.

PRATHA is drawn to objects with that potential. The goal is not to fill a room or a wardrobe quickly, but to choose pieces that have somewhere to go as life changes.

Good design can be quiet. Sometimes its greatest achievement is becoming part of the background of a life well lived.
