Why Chiniya?

A study in silk, light and the quiet character of Varanasi.

Chiniya begins with a simple attraction: the way silk catches light. In Varanasi, that quality becomes part of a much larger textile tradition — one shaped by patient hands, repetition and generations of knowledge.

For PRATHA, the appeal is not only the finished cloth. It is the feeling of wearing something whose material carries evidence of how it was made.

The first collection takes that idea into contemporary life: pieces that can be worn often, styled simply and kept long enough to become familiar.
