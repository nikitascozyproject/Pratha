The hand behind the weave.

On makers, repetition and the human rhythm inside Indian textiles.

A textile is never only a pattern. Behind every repeated motif is a sequence of decisions, movements and learned gestures.

That human element is one of the reasons PRATHA looks closely at the making process. The hand is visible in small variations — the things that keep an object from feeling anonymous.

We want to keep that relationship between maker and material visible as the brand grows.
